About

I have spent my career where software meets moving equipment: designing IoT devices that capture and ship sensor data, commissioning warehouse control software on large fulfilment sites, and now working on the controls of automated storage systems.

Every one of those systems produces a constant stream of events, alarms, timings and throughput figures. Most of it is used to keep the system running, and very little of it is used to improve the business. That gap is why I'm studying data science: to turn operational data from sorters, shuttles and storage systems into better metrics, new applications and better-informed product decisions.

I'm looking to collaborate with teams already working on data, analytics or AI in material handling.
